- Yes on both: SICC judgments are treated as awards, and like a tribunal it cannot bind non-parties || The SICC is a division of the General Division. Its judgments are court judgments, and it can join non-parties.
- Half right: its judgments are enforced as awards, but it can join Inca as a court || SICC judgments are Singapore High Court judgments, enforced abroad as court judgments, not awards.
* Wrong on both: it is a court, so its judgments are court judgments and it can join Inca || Correct: s 18A. Appeals lie to the Court of Appeal, and joinder is a key advantage over arbitration.
- Wrong on enforcement, but right that it cannot join non-signatories without their consent || As a court, the SICC can coerce a non-party into the proceedings by joinder (s 18D(3)).
