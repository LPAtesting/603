LAW302 Exam Day Sim

Twenty-four hypotheticals, each on one page with three panes: the question, your answer, and the LAW302 Menu exactly as it prints. Built for a wide screen.

  1. Read the question and fill the order slip at the top of your answer by clicking each key field (C, D, FC, K1 …).
  2. Tick the slip in the Menu (Slip Q1 or Slip Q2): one click is a tick, a second click is a cross on a box with a cross line.
  3. Drag a Menu block into your answer (or double-click it), press its K1 / K2 / FS button to fill the slots, and type only the analysis.
  4. Finish the part to see the model answer, grouped by contract, with every change from the Menu marked.

Each model answer was written from the reviewed drill answer and checked by an independent reviewer against the Menu and the pre-writes. Your work is saved in this browser, separately for each hypo.
