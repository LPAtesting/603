LAW302 · Conflict of Laws

Exam Day Sim

Start with the primer, then work the hypos. Each hypo is one page with three panes: the question, your answer, and your references, the LAW302 Menu and the Archetypes booklet exactly as they print (a tab switches between them). Built for a wide screen.

  1. Read the question and fill the order slip at the top of your answer by clicking each key field (C, D, FC, K1 …).
  2. Find the archetype for each contract or claim in the Archetypes tab, or tick the Menu's slip (Slip Q1 or Slip Q2).
  3. Drag a block in from either tab (or double-click it), press its K1 / K2 / FS button to fill the slots, and type only the analysis.
  4. Watch the clock: one budget for the hypo, one for the part you are on. Finish the part to see the model answer.
