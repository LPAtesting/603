Q1, per contract: [Claimant] sues [defendant] in [Singapore] on [contract], whose [clause X] (concluded [date], governed by [law]) names [the courts of State]. Which regime, and who is in breach?

Three regimes: the General Division at common law (J2p7–J4p19), the CCAA (J1.2p3–J1.4p4) and the SICC (J5p24). The CCAA displaces both others within its scope; the SICC displaces Spiliada for cases heard there. "Not every Convention case will be an SICC case, and not every SICC case will be a Convention case" (Yeo). At common law, ask two questions: is anyone in breach, and who bears the burden?
