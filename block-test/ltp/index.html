Which blocks would you open first?

One game for each of the 60 mock exam questions. Each question shows seven blocks from your exam sheet, already partly filled in for that question. Pick the best three, then check.

How it works
  • Seven options, pick the best 3. Each option is a sheet block already filled in for this question, about 50 words, labelled with its code and archetype number. The options are deliberately close: most are blocks you could use, but only three are the best, usually one for each main limb of the question.
  • Colours. Italic (green on the page) is copied unchanged from the sheet; bold (amber on the page) is changed for this question; "…" marks sheet text left out. Nothing is crossed out.
  • Check. Your score is out of 3. Each option you did not need is marked Close (usable for a line, but not top 3) or Wrong (a lure: it looks relevant but answers something else), with a short reason. One compiled reason explains the best three, ending with the answer guide's verdict line.
  • Scores are saved in your browser, and the question list shows your best score for each question.
