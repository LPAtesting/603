Why these three

What the question is testing. A one-line Chng statement. "The same" is either false, since all law classifies, or empty, since everyone can be treated equally badly. Give concrete illustrations.

"Equality before the law": 11.3. Art 12 asks for an intelligible line rationally related to the object. TSH's reply is that the question is which differences are morally significant, so equality needs a moral content.

"Treating everyone the same": 3.5. Fuller shows the formal idea is thin: like cases alike can be satisfied by any standard at all. Mandatory sentences are the local illustration, just only if the line tracks real differences.

"Do you agree?": 8.8. Equality still has a floor. Where it is deliberately betrayed, Radbruch says the statute lacks the nature of law. Concede that equality is contested, and confine the limb to facial betrayal.

The close ones. 9.4 asks which differences matter, 6.1 gives equality in justification, and 10.1 adds equal access.

Verdict line (from the answer guide)

Equality before the law means treating relevant likes alike, and deciding what is relevant is a moral judgment; "the same" is either false, since all law classifies, or empty, since it is satisfied by treating everyone equally badly.
